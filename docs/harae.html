The flagship game of MitamaOS

HARAE祓

Noisy spirits are breaking your network. You learn real defences, carry them as paper talismans called ofuda, and slap the right one on the right spirit. Every attack is simulated on screen, and nothing is ever transmitted.

CONCEPT PREVIEW · NOT IN THE FIRMWARE YET
1

Learn a defence, earn a card

Every lesson you pass in Shugyo, the lesson app, hands you an ofuda. Pass "Wi-Fi recon" and you get Sealed Frames, the real fix for a Wi-Fi disconnect attack. What you study becomes what you fight with.

2

Read a card at a glance

Cards carry no words, only symbols, so they read on a tiny screen. Suit top left, cost dots top right, the spirit it targets in the middle, and the kind stamp at the bottom.

Sealed Frames: a Wind card, costs 1, targets the Kamaitachi, a Seal.

3

Pick your hand

Before a drill you see which spirits are coming. The Seals you need go in your hand automatically, so the drill is always winnable. You add support cards, and a few of those are drawn at random each round.

4

Play a round

A round starts with a symptom, the thing a user would report. You have 3 action points.

  • Investigate: spend points to read clues such as logs or a spectrum view.
  • Surge: the outage keeps going while you look. Every point you spend also costs 5 uptime.
  • Play a card: the right Seal binds the spirit. The wrong card only half-works and costs you uptime or data.
  • Riichi: feeling sure? Press R before reading anything. Your next card is locked in, and if you're right it pays big. If you're wrong, the round just counts as partial.

The orange sliver on Uptime shows what Surge will take if you read one more clue.

5

Score: Purity × Resonance

Purity is how cleanly you won: the Seal's value, plus the uptime you kept, plus Prayer (every point you didn't spend). Resonance is how stylishly you won: it starts at 1 and grows with every combo you complete.

Purity 46×Resonance 3.5=161
  • Riichi: called it before reading evidence, +2
  • Clear Sky: every round stopped on the first play
  • Dora: played today's lucky card, marked with a gold corner
  • Kindred: a Seal and a Blessing from the same pantheon together

A sloppy win, where you read everything and make no combos, scores about 20. Chasing the gap is the game.

6

Meet Babel, the first faction

A rabble of lesser spirits from every pantheon, causing outages just because it's fun. Not every outage is them, though. Sometimes it's just the microwave.

7

Then go deeper

Clear your Domain and the Deep Aether opens: a roguelike descent through the ruins of the Fallen Tower. Your deck grows as you draft new cards at shrines between floors, and Charms bend the rules into bigger and bigger scores.

The fairness promise. Every drill can be won with the Seals you've earned, always. Luck only changes your score. And everything you learn is a real-world defence.
