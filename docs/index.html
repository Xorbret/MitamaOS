MitamaOS

A spirit that watches the airwaves

A defensive, listen-only companion OS for the M5Stack Cardputer ADV. A sassy little demon called the Mitama rides along, names every tracker, camera and attack tool it hears, logs the networks you pass, and teaches you real defences you then play as a card game. It detects and informs. It never attacks.

Meet the Mitama 御霊

The Mitama is a faceless spirit that lives on the home screen, Proxima. It shows the network you're on, what the watcher is hearing and the latest sightings, and it talks. Its mood rides the status bar, its aura shows how it feels, and it has opinions about everything you do.

A demon with a mouth on it

Sarcastic, a little theatrical, and annoyingly right. It drops lines from myth, old RPGs and tabletop games, peeks through the fourth wall now and then, gets sleepy late at night and tired after long sessions. Only real threats make it serious.

"Drone overhead. Huginn and Muninn, reporting to some Odin."

Forms and ki

23 forms unlock as you explore: spirit flames, fox ears, oni horns, a dragon coiled round the ring. Press F on Proxima to change. Everything you hear, log and learn earns ki: 50 levels across 10 ranks, and each rank changes the pattern of the Mitama's ring.

The apps

Fifteen tiles on a grid you drive from the keyboard: highlight with the arrows, Enter to open, ` to go back. No pointer, no desktop.

Hidden away on purpose: Proxima is home, not a tile. System info, Clock and Tsukumo live inside Settings. Tsukumo scans the Grove port for M5Stack modules read-only, names about 130 of them, and shows live readings from common sensors (temperature, CO₂, light, distance, RFID card IDs and more). Settings are backed up to the SD card so updates never wipe them.

The Compendium

Every device the watcher recognises is a demon, sorted into races. The Compendium is the bestiary: a page per species, filled in the first time you meet one. Unmet species show as ???. Fiends, the attack tools, are drawn wrong on purpose and never hold still.

Shugyo and Harae 修行 · 祓

Shugyo is the study app: short lessons you read, a Lua task the device grades, a quiz, and an optional field task. Pass a lesson and you earn its defence as an ofuda, a paper talisman card. Harae is where you use them: spirits from four pantheons hex your Domain, and you read the symptom, gather clues and play the Seal that binds them. Every board can be won if you hold the right cards. Everything is simulated on screen and nothing is transmitted.

Install

Every push to main builds the firmware and publishes it here. The current build is main. You need a Cardputer ADV. Scry's GPS and Kotodama's chat also need the Cap LoRa-1262.

Recommended · keeps your other firmware

From the SD card with Launcher

  1. Run Launcher on the Cardputer.
  2. Download mitamaos-cardputer-adv-merged.bin and copy it to the SD card.
  3. In Launcher choose SD, pick the file and install.

If flash still has free space, Launcher adds a new slot instead of replacing the old one. Delete the old MitamaOS first.

Updating

Molt, on the device

Open Molt, press Enter. It checks this site, downloads the newest build to /MitamaOS/update/ on the SD card and restarts. Install it from Launcher > SD as above. Your settings and progress stay.

Fresh install · desktop Chrome or Edge

Web flasher

Plug in by USB-C and install from the browser in one click.

This erases the whole flash, Launcher and anything else on it.

Manual

esptool

Flash the merged image at 0x0:

esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x0 mitamaos-cardputer-adv-merged.bin

Also on the m0-test release.

House rules

MitamaOS is built for people who want to know what's around them, not to mess with anyone else.

  • Never sends deauths, beacons, jamming or any other attack.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth detection only listens.
  • Kotodama is a quiet LoRa node: it never relays and can't read private messages.
  • Module scanning only reads; it never writes to a relay, motor or bootloader.
  • Shugyo lessons run in a sandbox that can't transmit.
  • Ki is only earned by listening, logging and learning.
