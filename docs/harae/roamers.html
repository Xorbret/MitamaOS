THE NIGHT-ROAMERS

Nishācara · निशाचर · the Rākṣasa court of Lanka

The Ramayana calls the rakshasas nishācara, "those who walk at night". They change shape at will, they're strongest after dark, and their king Ravana rules from a city of gold built on what he has taken. They don't break your network or copy it. They watch it, and they carry off whatever they see.

Who they are

What they attack

Confidentiality. Everything keeps working and nothing looks fake. Your secrets just leave: messages, locations, photos, passwords.

Why

Desire. Ravana saw Sita and wanted her, so he took her. His court is the same: they covet what's private because it's private.

How they act

Patient and quiet, and in the dark. A lure first, then the snatch. They're a real court with a king, brothers and generals, and they work to a plan.

Where they fit

After Babel's noise and the Neighbours' copies, the Night-Roamers just listen. The Fae wanted your logins. Ravana wants everything those logins protect.

Their rules

Leak

Babel drains uptime. The Night-Roamers drain data. Every round a spirit stays unsealed, 5 data leaks away, and leaked data can't be won back. What's encrypted is worthless to them, so encryption cards stop the bleed.

Fair by rule: playing the right Seal on the first round always leaves you with data to spare.

The Boon

Brahma granted Ravana a boon: no god, demon or spirit of heaven could kill him. He didn't bother asking for protection from humans or animals. So Blessings do nothing against his court, but Shikigami, your paper animals, work fine, just as Hanuman and the monkey army did.

Blessings were never needed to win, so this only changes your score.

The Lure

Once per drill a golden clue appears, cheaper and shinier than the rest. It's Maricha's deer. Chase it and you leave your hand unguarded for a round, and the court acts freely. It's always marked in gold, so you can learn to let it run.

Later waves

In the Deep Aether: Lanka

The golden city on its island, where every stolen secret is kept in the vaults. Data doesn't refill between floors, so a leak on floor one is still gone on floor five. The court's generals guard the gates: Indrajit fights from inside the clouds and Kumbhakarna sleeps under the walls until something wakes him. Rahu circles overhead as an elite. Halfway down, a Shikigami run can do what Hanuman did and set the city alight: clear a floor without a single leak and the next floor's vault burns open.
