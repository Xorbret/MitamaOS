Harae · faction 4 · Abrahamic scripture and lore

THE FALLEN

Shedim · the Watchers · Jinn and Shayatin · Legion

Angels who came down and taught forbidden arts. Spirits Solomon bound with a ring and set to building the Temple. A tenth of the demons left on earth by agreement, to lead humans astray. A spirit in a man among the tombs who answered, "My name is Legion, for we are many." The other factions wanted a reaction, your logins or your secrets. The Fallen want you: your devices, working for them, for as long as they can hold on.

Who they are

What they attack

Control. Everything still works, but not for you any more. Your laptop mines for someone else, your account answers to a new password, your camera joins a swarm.

Why

Possession. In every one of these texts, the demons want a body to live in or a soul to bind by contract. A device is a body, and a ransom note is a contract.

How they act

They organise. The Goetia ranks seventy-two spirits as kings, dukes and presidents, each with legions under them. They move in, dig in, and leave something behind when you throw them out.

Where they fit

The last district. Everything the earlier factions stole (handshakes, passwords, secrets) is how the Fallen get inside. Beat them and the way up opens.

Their rules

Possession

Every round a spirit stays unsealed, it possesses one support card in your hand. A possessed card shows the spirit's sigil and can't be played. Seal the spirit and every card it held is freed.

Fair by rule: Seals can never be possessed.

Name and angel

In the Testament of Solomon, every demon Solomon questions must tell him its name and the angel who thwarts it. Spend 2 points to interrogate a spirit and it confesses exactly which Seal binds it. Expensive, and certain.

Testament of Solomon

A tenth remain

In Jubilees, Mastema begs God to leave him a tenth of the spirits, and God agrees. Here, a sealed spirit leaves a remnant that comes back one round later, weaker, unless you clean up with the right Temizu. Persistence is the whole faction.

Later waves

In the Deep Aether: the ascent

Every other realm goes down. This one goes up. In the Gnostic texts the world is ruled by Archons, and a soul leaving it has to climb past their gates one heaven at a time, giving each gatekeeper the right answer or being turned back.

So each floor of the ascent is a gate with an Archon on it, and the Archon asks a question from a lesson you passed in Shugyo. Answer and you climb. Miss it and you fight that floor with one less card. Leviathan coils around the lower heavens as an elite. This is the point of no return: from here, the run only ends at the top.

At the top sits Yaldabaoth, the lion-faced serpent of the Apocryphon of John: the flawed creator who made the world, rules it through the Archons, and declares, "I am God and there is no other God beside me." In Harae he's the last boss of the whole game: compromised firmware, the layer underneath everything, which tells every other layer that it's fine.
